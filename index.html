    Your keys

    The rules

    • Every player drives a light cycle that leaves a solid trail behind it.
    • You have two keys: turn left and turn right, relative to where you're heading.
    • Hit a wall, the edge or any trail (yours too) and you're out.
    • The last one alive wins the round and scores a point. The first to 10 points wins the match.
    • During the 3-2-1 countdown you can already turn to pick your starting direction.
    • Space starts the next round, Esc opens the menu and the ? button brings up this page.
    • It's best with friends: up to 8 players, sharing one keyboard or online.
    • Playing with someone much younger or newer to the game? Try Catch-up mode: the more rounds you've won, the faster your line goes.
